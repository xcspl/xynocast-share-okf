# Case studies

A selection of engagements across our service areas. Most client
identities are kept out; we name a client only where the work is already
public. The results speak for themselves.

## Application development & digital marketing

- **B2B content marketing** — content strategy for a software company
- **Coffee chain social media** — hyperlocal, geofenced ad campaigns
- **Serious game for safety training** — a gamified industrial-safety
  simulation built in Unity/Unreal, improving training retention
- **Tezpur Mahila Samiti** — a new website for a women's organisation in
  Tezpur, Assam, with more than a century of history: its story and photo
  archive brought onto a fast, search-friendly static site
  (tezpurmahilasamiti.org)

## ERP & CRM

- **Manufacturing ERP** — a Zoho ERP/CRM integration that resolved
  recurring production-scheduling bottlenecks
- **Odoo ERP for e-commerce** — order-processing time cut in half
- **Hybrid ERP for manufacturing** — a Zoho backend paired with a custom
  frontend, built for low-friction adoption on the shop floor

## Digitization & analytics

- **Predictive analytics in finance** — ML-based forecasting, a 40%
  accuracy improvement over the prior process
- **Retail customer segmentation** — cohort analytics driving a 30%
  increase in engagement
- **Manufacturing process automation** — IoT-based logging, cutting
  errors by half
- **Fintech bill discounting** — loan origination reduced from days to
  hours

## IT administration & DevOps

- **DevOps for financial services** — infrastructure as code, cutting
  outages by 40%
- **Enterprise CI/CD** — a Jenkins/GitLab pipeline that reduced deployment
  failures by half
- **On-prem to AWS migration** — a zero-downtime cloud migration

## Innovation management

- **Southeast Asia innovation pipeline** — a stage-gate funnel that
  launched three products
- **Product line revitalization** — sprint workshops that reversed a
  declining product's churn
- **Skincare go-to-market** — an influencer-led launch campaign that beat
  sales targets by 20%

## Conservation & AI platforms

- **EarthTeam** — a live conservation platform: mobile, web, and
  geospatial API, which we build and operate end to end under contract for
  Freeland, which owns it.
- **LLMAgentO** — our multi-tenant AI platform: agents with hybrid search
  over their own knowledge, and a model gateway with failover, running on
  Cloudflare Workers. Trelvia AI and Sitehub are built on it.
