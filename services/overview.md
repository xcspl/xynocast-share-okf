# Services

## Application & web development

Custom development across web, Android, iOS, and game engines. Full-stack
web on Astro, Django, and Next.js. 50+ websites built, hosted, and
maintained across NGOs, manufacturers, retailers, e-commerce, and more,
with payment-gateway integration and GIS/map-based applications.

## NGO & conservation technology

Full-platform delivery anchored by our own [EarthTeam](/products/overview.md)
build — mobile, web, and geospatial API, with gamified field verification
and contributor progression.

## Web hosting & domain services

Web and email hosting, domain registration and reseller, Google Workspace
setup and management, DNS, SSL, and CDN. Contact and enquiry forms, and a
site's outbound mail, run on our own [XynoForms](/products/overview.md).

## IT administration & DevOps

Cloud infrastructure across AWS, Azure, and GCP; CI/CD; containerization
and orchestration; infrastructure as code; monitoring; security and
compliance automation. 100+ active accounts under active management.

## ERP & CRM

Odoo Learning Partner. ERPNext implementation and custom hybrid ERPs — a
simplified frontend on a standard ERP core, when that's the right fit. Own
products [Trelvia AI and Clearent](/products/overview.md) — Trelvia puts a
conversational layer over the ERP or CRM a business already runs.

## Digitization & analytics

AI/ML including NLP, computer vision, and custom classifiers; predictive
analytics; process automation; legacy-to-cloud migration; custom RAG
pipelines for document intelligence and enterprise chatbots.

## LLM infrastructure & AI platforms

Building and operating LLM infrastructure — our own platform, LLMAgentO:
AI agents with their own knowledge, a model gateway with failover across
providers, and Sitehub, where websites are edited by chat. See
[Products](/products/overview.md).

## Innovation management

Go-to-market strategy, stage-gate innovation pipelines, and idea-to-launch
workshops for product teams.

## Connectivity (Xynocast Fibernet)

Home and office broadband, IP CCTV, captive portals, network management,
and ISP consultation for regional connectivity providers.

See how these come together for clients in [Work](/work/index.md).
