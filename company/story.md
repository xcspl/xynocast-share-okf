# Our story

Xynocast began in 2013 in Whitefield, Bangalore, as a wireless technology
and network-integration operation serving local organizations. A year in,
we partnered with **Railtel Corporation of India**, a Government of India
enterprise, to help bring affordable connectivity to underserved parts of
northeast India — a partnership that continues to this day.

That connectivity work led us deep into infrastructure: designing GPON
fibre networks and building the MPLS backbones that regional ISPs run on.
By 2016 we'd opened a software-development practice for mobile and web,
and by 2018 the connectivity and software sides of the business had grown
enough to become distinct companies — **Xynocast Fibernet** for networking
and fibre, and the software and consultancy practice that would later
become **Xynocast Consultancy Services Pvt Ltd (XCSPL)**.

The years since have added machine learning, AR/VR, and enterprise
consultancy to the mix, and most recently **LLMAgentO**, our AI platform,
and the products built on it — **Trelvia AI** and **Sitehub**. That's the
current edge of where we're taking the group.

Through all of it, one constant: we've grown on client revenue and
reinvestment, never outside capital. Every product in our line today
exists because it paid for itself.
