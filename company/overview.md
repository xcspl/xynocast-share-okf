# About Xynocast

> *"Technology based solutions for Tomorrow."*

**Xynocast** is a technology group built the hard way: no institutional
venture capital, no slideware, over a decade of compounding real-world
delivery. Founded in 2013 in Bangalore and now headquartered in Guwahati,
Assam, we grew from a wireless connectivity and networking operation into a
multi-vertical group spanning application development, DevOps and cloud
infrastructure, ERP/CRM, data analytics and AI, LLM infrastructure, and
fibre broadband — under two operating companies, **Xynocast Consultancy
Services Pvt Ltd (XCSPL)** and **Xynocast Fibernet Pvt Ltd**.

## What sets us apart

Everything in our portfolio is **in-hand, working technology** — not pitch
decks. A live conservation platform serving real field data. A multi-agent
RAG platform with its own LLM proxy gateway, built and operated end to end.
A vertical ERP product in active rollout. A healthcare digital-twin
platform taking shape. An AI-native hosting platform reimagining how
websites get built and maintained. We ship, then we keep operating what we
ship.

Being bootstrapped isn't a footnote — it's the reason our roadmap answers
to clients and to the market, not to a fundraising calendar.
