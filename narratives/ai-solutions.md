# Our AI story

**The labs make intelligence. We make it do the work.**

## The power plant

The big AI labs, like Anthropic, OpenAI and DeepSeek, make the
intelligence: GPUs, training, data, research. What comes out is tokens, sold
by the million. That's the power plant. And power on its own doesn't do
anything.

## The grid — LLMAgentO

[LLMAgentO](/products/overview.md) makes that power usable and manageable.
Usable: agents that work on files, code and tools, with their own knowledge,
memory, a data store and voice. Manageable: one gateway in front of many
labs, failover when one has a bad day, and keys, projects and costs under
control. Sitehub runs on the same grid — static sites and online stores
their owners change by chatting.

## The appliance — Trelvia AI

[Trelvia AI](/products/overview.md) sits over the ERP or CRM a business
already runs, like Tally, Zoho or Odoo, and does the work. Staff send a
message instead of filling in forms. Leads get handled, books get kept,
customers get answered.

## Why we build it this way

We're independent of every lab, so each job goes to the best and cheapest
lab for it, and we're free to switch. It's built once: every new product
starts with agents, data and voice already in place. And it stays close to
the work, because the value is in what gets done, not in the tokens.

## The safe layer under your work

Now everyone can code. With a coding agent, anyone can build their own app,
agent or automation. What's hard to see is the security holes, the backups
nobody set up, the data that can be lost, the query that falls over at
scale, the bill that quietly triples.

That's where our background comes in: hardware, networking, an ISP of our
own, servers, databases, backend and DevOps. We're the layer underneath your
work, so things go wrong less often, get caught early, and your data stays
recoverable. See [Services](/services/overview.md).
