# Xynocast — Public-Facing Knowledge Base

A curated, promotional-voice knowledge base about Xynocast for prospective
employees and clients. **This is a pure knowledge dir**: the whole directory
is one OKF bundle.

> **This bundle is not the source of truth.** It exists to present Xynocast
> well, not to record it accurately in full. The company's real system of
> record is `xynocast-okf` (internal, private, not distributed externally).
> Where this bundle and `xynocast-okf` disagree, `xynocast-okf` is correct.
> Content policy — what may and may never appear here — is governed from
> `xynocast-okf/sops/external-knowledge-sharing.md`.
>
> This repo is git-backed for our own editing convenience (marketing team
> included). External recipients never get repo access — they receive a
> point-in-time export with no `.git` history. See
> [meta/maintenance.md](meta/maintenance.md) for both procedures.
